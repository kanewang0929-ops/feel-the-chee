05 / REPLAY PROTOCOL

Same archive. Separate minds.

  1. Seed independenceEach of the 100 simulations receives a unique deterministic seed and starts from isolated agent state.
  2. Chronological integrityPredictions are sealed before the target draw is revealed. No future draw may enter the training prefix.
  3. One official ticketJoy and Kane each submit exactly five front numbers and two back numbers per draw.
  4. Audit every replayPrediction, result and audit ledgers are cross-checked for every historical draw in every simulation.

06 / SAFETY RAILS

What this dashboard does not claim

  • Past frequency or overdue numbers do not increase a number’s probability in a fair independent draw.
  • The highest-scoring simulation is not automatically a better model.
  • Daily batches do not alter the live prediction lock or live model state.
  • No model is promoted from these full-history replay scores alone.
Simulation mode is isolated from live prediction mode.
