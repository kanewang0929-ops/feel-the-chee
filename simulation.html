HOW LIVE TRAINING WORKS

Learning is separate from simulation

The live prediction workflow scores sealed historical predictions after each reveal and updates each agent's independent signal weights. The daily optimizer searches up to one million candidate weight combinations per agent, subject to its 23-hour minimum interval. Simulation replays do not write to these live states.

EXPLICIT APPROVAL REQUIRED

Promotion gate

A candidate must have a traceable model-state package, pass chronological and leakage checks, include a matched random-baseline evaluation, and receive human approval. No simulation batch can promote a model by itself.

  1. PrepareProduce a candidate model-state package from a documented training run.
  2. EvaluateRun chronological holdout evaluation and compare against the matched random baseline.
  3. ReviewInspect the evidence, weight changes and prediction-lock compatibility.
  4. ApproveApprove a dedicated promotion change before it is merged into the live model files.

05 / REPLAY PROTOCOL

Same archive. Separate minds.

  1. Seed independenceEach of the 100 simulations receives a unique deterministic seed and starts from isolated agent state.
  2. Chronological integrityPredictions are sealed before the target draw is revealed. No future draw may enter the training prefix.
  3. One official ticketJoy and Kane each submit exactly five front numbers and two back numbers per draw.
  4. Audit every replayPrediction, result and audit ledgers are cross-checked for every historical draw in every simulation.

06 / SAFETY RAILS

What this dashboard does not claim

  • Past frequency or overdue numbers do not increase a number’s probability in a fair independent draw.
  • The highest-scoring simulation is not automatically a better model.
  • Daily batches do not alter the live prediction lock or live model state.
  • No model is promoted from these full-history replay scores alone.
Simulation mode is isolated from live prediction mode.
