ONE TRAINING PATH

Predict. Reveal. Score. Learn.

Each cycle starts from its own initial Joy and Kane states and traverses the archive in order. The first 200 draws are learned as warm-up and excluded from reported hit scores. Every later draw has one sealed ticket per agent before its result is revealed. Parameters update only inside this process.

Joy uses a quantum circuit Born machine. Kane uses a split-step quantum walk. Both run as classical simulations. The training objective is exact unordered-ticket negative log likelihood after reveal; the public score remains front hits plus back hits.

After all 100 cycles pass, the fixed final cycle supplies the next model checkpoint. No best-scoring seed or ensemble is selected. The separate optimizer and research trainer are retired.

05 / REPLAY PROTOCOL

Same archive. Separate minds.

  1. Seed independenceEach of the 100 simulations receives a unique deterministic seed and starts from isolated agent state.
  2. One complete cycleTraverse the full archive in chronological order: seal predictions, reveal the result, score, update, then continue. The first 200 draws update the models as warm-up and are not scored.
  3. Chronological integrityPredictions use only earlier draws. No future draw may enter the training prefix.
  4. One official ticketJoy and Kane each submit exactly five front numbers and two back numbers per draw.
  5. Audit every replayPrediction, result and audit ledgers are cross-checked for every historical draw in every simulation.

06 / SAFETY RAILS

What this dashboard does not claim

  • Quantum mathematics does not reveal the outcome of a fair, independent random draw.
  • The fixed final cycle is used regardless of which cycle scored highest.
  • A ready batch cannot replace a prediction whose actual result is still pending.
  • Historical replay scores and prospective live results are different evidence.
Training happens only inside the 100 cycles; the live publisher only compares, checks and predicts.
